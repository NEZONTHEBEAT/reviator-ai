Why now?

Revenue loss rarely happens in one clean step. A payment degrades, a checkout gets abandoned, a subscription fails, or an invoice goes overdue. Reviator closes the loop — from detecting the problem, to diagnosing it, to choosing and executing the right intervention.

What Reviator recovers

Seven directions the same detect–decide–recover loop applies to.

Payment degradation → root cause

Card declines and retries traced back to their real cause, then routed to the right fix.

Checkout drop-off recovery

Abandoned checkouts get a timed nudge, before the buying intent is gone.

Failed-subscription recovery

Dunning that adapts — retry windows tuned to when a card is actually likely to work.

B2B receivables chaser

Overdue invoices followed up on a schedule your finance team would call fair.

Mandate retry sequencer

NACH / UPI Autopay mandate failures retried on a compliant schedule, not a guess.

Hinglish voice recovery

A calling agent that speaks the way your customers actually do.

Promise-to-pay tracker

Every commitment a customer makes gets logged, watched, and followed up.
